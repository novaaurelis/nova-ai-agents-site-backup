# First steps

**Why build a team that will move you forward?**

## What is this thing

OpenClaw is a modern digital assistant that differs from a typical chat bot not only in the quality of answers but in the whole approach to work. A regular chat bot mostly holds a conversation: it answers questions, explains and suggests. OpenClaw is designed as a tool that gets tasks done: it acts on user commands, integrates with external services, uses the browser and automations, and helps carry a process through to a result.

## Not just another chat bot

The key difference in OpenClaw is its agent logic. The system doesn't stop at a single reply — it builds a sequence of actions: breaks a task into steps, chooses tools and executes work. Instead of only explaining, the agent can collect information, open required sites, process data and prepare messages. OpenClaw is closer to a digital assistant that performs routine operations.

## Free yourself from manual repetitive work

OpenClaw focuses on practical automation: regularity, speed and repeatability. It's useful for handling repetitive requests, supporting communications, monitoring events and executing scheduled actions — both for personal use and for business.

## Limitless possibilities

First, you can work with multiple AI models: pick local or cloud, fast or precise. Second, integrations with platforms and messengers make the agent available where you work. Third, access to tools — browser, files, system commands — turns a text assistant into a full-fledged executor.

## Context and memory

OpenClaw can preserve context, take previous steps and the user's goal into account. Unlike a standard model, an agent can build complex scenarios — from data gathering to step‑by‑step actions in interfaces.

## TL;DR

OpenClaw is not just a chat bot but a digital secretary that connects conversational interface with real actions. Its strength is helping to perform work: from hints to automation, from text to real value.

## Team of agents

With OpenClaw you can create a whole team of agents: an information gatherer, an analyst, a copywriter, a scheduler/executor. Together they act like a coordinated system, saving time and resources.
